## FAQ

Q: We just need a writer.
A: Then hire a writer. Copy without positioning under it is how sites end up saying "AI-powered platform for modern teams". If your positioning is already sharp, I will just write, and it costs less.

Q: We are hiring a PMM.
A: Good. I will build the foundation and hand it to them with a walkthrough. Most first PMMs spend their first quarter doing exactly the Positioning Sprint anyway.

Q: You do not know our product.
A: I will after the first week. I interview engineers and read the docs. That is faster than teaching a generalist agency what a CI pipeline is.

Q: Do you use AI?
A: Yes, for research, competitor monitoring and first drafts. Interviews, positioning decisions and final copy are mine. You will not get generic AI output, because that is exactly what your competitors publish.
