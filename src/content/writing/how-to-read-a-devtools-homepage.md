## The five questions

1. Can I say what it does in one sentence after the hero?
2. Do I know who it is for, specifically enough to rule myself out?
3. What does it replace? Is the alternative named, or only implied?
4. Is there one claim a competitor could not make?
5. Is the proof specific (numbers, names, code) or decorative (logos, adjectives)?
